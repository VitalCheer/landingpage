# Cookie Policy

> **INTERNAL · DRAFT · REQUIRES LEGAL REVIEW.**
> The VitalCheer website currently sets **no cookies** and uses no browser storage, analytics, advertising
> pixels or session recording (checked 11 November 2026 against `index.html`, `script.js` and `js/scenes3d.js`).
> The public Privacy Policy already covers this (section 15). Publish this separate policy **only if**
> cookies or similar technologies are introduced, for example a dashboard sign-in or analytics.
