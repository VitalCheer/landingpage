# Clinical Disclaimer and Intended Use Statement

> **INTERNAL · DRAFT · REQUIRES LEGAL AND REGULATORY REVIEW.**
> The wording of an intended-use statement can determine whether a product is regulated as a medical device,
> and in which risk class. It must be reviewed by a medical-device regulatory specialist before it appears on
> labels, in instructions for use, or in marketing.

## 1. Product status (to be stated wherever the product is described)

VitalCheer is a **prototype** IoT IV monitoring system in development. It has **not** been clinically validated,
and has **not** been registered, certified, cleared or approved by any regulatory authority. It is not
commercially available.

## 2. Draft intended-use statement (prototype evaluation only)

> The VitalCheer prototype is an investigational system intended for use by authorised nurses and healthcare
> professionals, under clinical supervision, during approved evaluation activities, to observe gravity IV
> infusion parameters, flow measurements and device status, and to receive system-generated alerts.
> It is intended to be used **in addition to**, and not instead of, standard clinical monitoring,
> clinical judgement and approved medical equipment.

## 5. Clinical disclaimer (standard text)

The VitalCheer prototype does not replace professional clinical judgement, existing clinical supervision,
hospital protocols or approved medical equipment. Information it shows may be inaccurate, delayed or
unavailable, and it may fail to alert. The absence of an alert does not mean the absence of a problem.
Any calculated dose or flow rate must be independently verified by a qualified professional.
