# Data Breach and Incident Response Policy

> **INTERNAL · DRAFT · REQUIRES LEGAL AND TECHNICAL REVIEW.**
> Notification deadlines and authority contacts must be confirmed against the law in force
> (Sri Lanka's Personal Data Protection Act No. 9 of 2022 as amended by Act No. 22 of 2025, and the GDPR or HIPAA
> only where they apply) and against each partner's DPA. Version 0.1 · 11 November 2026.

## 1. Scope

Security incidents and personal data breaches affecting the website, enquiries inbox, repositories, devices,
dashboard, backend or cloud services, including incidents at our providers.

**Clinical safety incidents** (for example a device giving wrong readings during testing) also follow the
testing site's incident procedures and the Prototype Testing Agreement.

## 3. Response steps

1. **Report.** Anyone who suspects an incident reports it immediately to the incident lead via [CHANNEL]. Do not delete evidence.
2. **Contain.** Revoke credentials, isolate affected systems or devices, pause data flows, and pause testing if patient safety could be affected.
3. **Assess.** What happened, which data and data subjects, whether health data is involved, the likelihood and severity of harm.
4. **Notify (if required).**
   - Healthcare organisation (controller): without undue delay, within [HOURS PER DPA].
   - Data Protection Authority of Sri Lanka: [TIMEFRAME AND CRITERIA, TO BE CONFIRMED UNDER PDPA AS AMENDED].
   - Affected individuals: where required, usually through or in coordination with the healthcare organisation.
   - Other authorities (e.g. EU/UK supervisory authority within 72 hours where GDPR applies; HIPAA breach notification only where VitalCheer is a business associate). [LEGAL REVIEW]
5. **Recover.** Restore from clean backups, fix the root cause, verify.
6. **Learn.** A post-incident review within [DAYS]; update controls and this policy.

## 4. Incident log

Keep a record of every incident, including those not notified: date, description, data affected, decisions and reasons, actions taken.
