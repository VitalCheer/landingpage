03Description of Services

The Services consist of:

  • The public website, which provides information about VitalCheer and a contact form for enquiries.
  • The VitalCheer prototype system, which is being developed to include:
    • a physical IoT device designed to attach to a standard gravity IV line;
    • Bluetooth connectivity between the device and supported software;
    • a web-based monitoring dashboard;
    • a backend platform for processing and managing monitoring information; and
    • monitoring of IV fluid parameters, flow measurements and device status, together with system-generated alerts.

Features described on the website, including automated flow control, early infiltration detection, dose calculation, central dashboards and mobile alerting, describe the intended design of the system. Not every feature may be implemented, available or working as described at any given time.

06Medical and Clinical Disclaimer

Important

VitalCheer is a prototype. It is not an approved or certified medical device and must not be used as the sole basis for any clinical decision, or in place of standard clinical monitoring of a patient.

In particular, VitalCheer:

  • does not replace professional clinical judgement, existing clinical supervision, your organisation's protocols, or approved and certified medical equipment;
  • must not be relied on to start, stop, adjust or monitor a patient's infusion instead of standard clinical practice;
  • may show information (including flow rates, fluid volumes, device status, alerts and calculated values) that is inaccurate, delayed, incomplete or unavailable;
  • may fail to raise an alert. The absence of an alert does not mean the absence of a problem; and
  • provides dose and flow-rate calculations only as an aid. Any calculated value must be independently checked by a qualified professional before it is used.

Healthcare professionals remain fully responsible for patient care in line with their professional obligations and their organisation's policies. In an emergency, follow your organisation's emergency procedures.

Content on the public website is general information about the project. It is not medical advice.
