01Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how [LEGAL ENTITY NAME] ("VitalCheer", "we", "us" or "our") handles personal data in connection with:

  • the public website, including its contact form; and
  • the VitalCheer prototype system, an IoT-based IV monitoring solution that is still in development.

To be clear about what happens today and what is still planned, this policy uses two labels:

  • Current: processing that happens through the public website now.
  • Planned: processing that the prototype system is being designed to carry out during approved testing. It may change before any testing begins.

02Information We Collect

We handle five kinds of information, each described in its own section:

  • Website enquiries that you send through the contact form (current), described in section 6.
  • Healthcare professional information about users of the prototype system (planned), described in section 3.
  • Patient-related information linked to monitored infusions (planned), described in section 4.
  • Device and monitoring information generated by VitalCheer devices (planned), described in section 5.
  • Technical and operational logs from the website and the prototype system (current and planned), described in section 6.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

03Healthcare Professional Information

Planned. When the prototype system is used in approved testing, we expect to process information about the nurses and healthcare professionals who use it, such as:

  • name, professional role and ward or department;
  • work email address and sign-in details issued for the prototype;
  • the organisation they work for; and
  • a record of actions taken in the dashboard (for example acknowledging an alert or changing infusion settings), so that activity can be traced and reviewed.

04Patient-Related Information

Planned. Monitoring an infusion may involve information about the patient receiving it, such as:

  • a patient name or identifier, bed number and ward;
  • infusion details, such as the type of fluid, prescribed volume, target duration and flow rate; and
  • alerts and events related to that patient's infusion.

Information about a person's health is a special or sensitive category of personal data under data protection laws, and we will treat it with particular care. In particular:

  • patient-related information will only be processed during approved testing under a written agreement with the healthcare organisation, which remains responsible for its patients' information;
  • we intend to collect only what is needed for monitoring, and to use pseudonymous identifiers (such as bed numbers) instead of names wherever practical; and
  • the prototype is not designed to collect patients' contact details, medical histories or diagnoses.

[CONFIRM WHETHER REAL PATIENT INFORMATION WILL BE PROCESSED DURING TESTING, OR ONLY SIMULATED OR ANONYMISED DATA]

05Device and Monitoring Information

Planned. VitalCheer devices are designed to generate information about the infusion and the device itself, such as:

  • drop counts, flow rate measurements and estimated fluid remaining;
  • sensor readings used to look for possible flow problems or infiltration;
  • device identifier, battery level, connection status and Bluetooth pairing details; and
  • timestamps and system-generated alerts.

On its own, this information describes the device and the infusion. When it is linked to a bed, ward or patient, it is treated as patient-related information.

06Website and Technical Information

Current. When you use the website:

  • Contact form. If you send an enquiry, we receive the name, professional email address and message you enter. The form is delivered through FormSubmit to our email inbox at vitalcheergroup@gmail.com, which is provided by Google (Gmail).
  • Fonts and libraries. Your browser loads fonts from Google Fonts and an open-source scrolling library from jsDelivr. Like any website request, this shares your IP address and basic browser information with those providers.
  • Hosting. Our hosting provider, [WEBSITE HOSTING PROVIDER], may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, the pages requested, date and time, and browser type.
  • No cookies or tracking. The website does not set its own cookies, does not store information in your browser, and does not use analytics, advertising pixels or session recording.

Planned. The prototype system is expected to keep technical and operational logs, such as error reports, security events and connection records, so that it can be operated, debugged and kept secure.

07How We Collect Information

We collect information:

  • directly from you, when you send an enquiry or contact us by email (current);
  • from healthcare organisations taking part in testing, which provide user accounts and infusion details (planned);
  • from VitalCheer devices and software, which generate monitoring information, alerts and logs (planned); and
  • automatically, through technical requests made when you visit the website (current).

08How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • reply to enquiries and arrange conversations, pilots or demonstrations (current);
  • operate the prototype system during approved testing, including showing monitoring information and sending alerts to authorised staff (planned);
  • evaluate, test and improve the prototype, using de-identified or aggregated information wherever practical (planned);
  • keep the website and prototype secure, and investigate faults or misuse (current and planned); and
  • meet legal, regulatory and contractual obligations (current and planned).

We do not use personal data for advertising.

The prototype is designed to raise alerts and, in its intended design, to pause an infusion automatically when it detects an irregularity. 09Legal Basis for Processing

VitalCheer is based in Sri Lanka. We aim to handle personal data in line with Sri Lanka's Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022, as amended by the Personal Data Protection (Amendment) Act, No. 22 of 2025, to the extent their provisions are in force, and with guidance issued by the Data Protection Authority of Sri Lanka.

Depending on the activity, we expect to rely on the following grounds:

  • Website enquiries: our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us, or steps you ask us to take before entering into an agreement.
  • Prototype testing with healthcare organisations: performance of our written agreements, and the conditions that apply to health data, which may include explicit consent or purposes connected with healthcare, as permitted by law.
  • Security and legal compliance: legal obligations and legitimate interests in keeping our services secure.

Where the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, for example if we offer services to people in the EU or UK, we will identify the applicable lawful basis under Article 6 and, for health data, a condition under Article 9.

The US Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) applies only to certain US healthcare organisations and their business associates. It would become relevant only if VitalCheer worked with such organisations, in which case a business associate agreement would be put in place.
