The clinical challenge

IV infusion shouldn't be a matter of guesswork.

Traditional IV systems rely heavily on manual adjustments, periodic observation, and human calculations. These limitations create avoidable risks for patients and additional pressure for healthcare professionals.

Scroll through the six challenges
00 / 06 Gravity IV line
  1. 01

    Unstable Flow Rates

    Precision fades over time.

    PVC tubing deformation and roller clamp drift.

  2. 02

    Manual Calculation Errors

    Every drop counts.

    Drop-factor conversion errors compound quickly.

  3. 03

    Administrative Overload

    Less paperwork. More patient care.

    Time spent verifying prescriptions and logging.

  4. 04

    Limited Ward-Wide Visibility

    You cannot monitor what you cannot see.

    No centralized multi-bed monitoring.

  5. 05

    Delayed Complication Detection

    A small leak can become serious.

    Infiltration and extravasation go unnoticed.

  6. 06

    The Cost of Smart Healthcare

    Advanced care shouldn't be out of reach.

    Expensive pumps and proprietary disposables.
