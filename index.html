IV infusion shouldn't be a matter of guesswork.

Traditional IV systems rely heavily on manual adjustments, periodic observation, and human calculations. These limitations create avoidable risks for patients and additional pressure for healthcare professionals.

Core innovations

One system. Every signal.

01

Automated Flow Control

Image slot · VITAL Medical Sensor System2.png

This feature dynamically regulates the infusion rate and instantly halts fluid delivery when an irregularity or blockage is identified. By removing the need for manual roller-clamp adjustments, it ensures precise delivery continuity and provides an immediate automated safety shutoff to protect the patient from flow errors.

02

Multi-Device Central Dashboard

Image slot · multi-device-central-dashboard.png

This aggregates live operational data from every active infusion device across the ward into a single, centralized interface. It gives nursing staff an instant, real-time overview of drip rates, remaining fluid volumes, and device battery levels for every bed, eliminating the need for constant manual checks and ensuring potential issues are spotted across the entire unit at a glance.

03

Early Infiltration Detection

Image slot · early-infiltration-detection.png

Continuously monitors fluid behavior and surrounding tissue response to identify early-stage fluid leakage before visible swelling or tissue damage occurs. By detecting subtle flow anomalies and localized physical changes at the infusion site, it automatically triggers safety protocols halting flow and alerting staff before painful subcutaneous buildup can develop.

04

Precision Dose Calculator

Image slot · precision-dose-calculator.png

Calculates optimal dosage amounts and precise infusion flow rates based on patient-specific parameters such as age, weight, and clinical condition. By replacing manual calculation steps at the bedside, it eliminates dangerous dosing errors, guarantees accurate flow speed recommendations, and streamlines the medication setup process for nursing staff.

Essential utilities

Quiet tools for better care.

Everyday clinical support, made visible and easy to act on.

  • Continuous Data Recording

    Image · images/continuous-data-recording.png

    Replaces tedious manual record-keeping by automatically capturing every infusion start time, volume change, and operational event in real time. This eliminates human error during data entry, saves valuable nursing time during shift logs, and maintains an accurate, digital timeline of the entire treatment process.

  • Battery & Low Liquid Alerts

    Image · images/battery-low-liquid-alerts.png

    Provides instant, automated warnings when device battery levels drop below operational thresholds or when liquid moisture is detected near sensitive electronic components. This ensures preemptive battery replacement before power failure occurs and protects the internal hardware from potential liquid exposure, safeguarding continuous device reliability during patient treatments.

  • Mobile Alerting Plus

    Image · images/mobile-alerting-plus.png

    Pushes clean, immediate push notifications directly to nursing staff mobile devices whenever an anomaly, low battery, or flow interruption occurs. By delivering patient name, bed number, and specific alert details straight to the caregiver's hands, it eliminates the need for stationary monitoring stations and ensures rapid response times from anywhere in the facility.
